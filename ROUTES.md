# Route variants

All distances are straight-line × 1.25 to approximate sidewalk routing, at 1.35 m/s.
Add roughly 5 minutes per stop for narration and discussion.

## V1 — Library start

**Gather:** Rugby Library · **End:** National Grid (or Footprints via spur)
**9 stops · 1.92 mi · ~38 min walking**

Civic gathering space with room for a presentation. Opens on the library's own retrofit story, meets the proposed hub site two minutes later, and builds from community scale out to utility scale.
